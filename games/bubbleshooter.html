About this game

Aim, shoot and pop your way through more than 1,000 handcrafted bubble levels in Bubble Shooter: Rainbow Pop.

Line up three or more bubbles of the same colour and watch them burst. Cut a cluster loose from the ceiling and the whole thing drops for bonus points.

Journey across colourful worlds with real bank shots off the walls, boosters, daily rewards and live events.

Quick sessions, smooth physics and bright visuals make it easy to pick up and hard to put down.

How to play

  1. Drag to aim, release to shoot.
  2. Match three or more bubbles of the same colour to pop them.
  3. Drop whole clusters by cutting them from the ceiling.
  4. Use boosters on tricky boards and clear the level for stars.
