About this game

Pakistanopoly is a fun, fast desi board game, a Pakistani twist on the classic Monopoly-style property game.

Roll the dice, tour the country, buy up cities, collect rent and bankrupt your rivals to become the ultimate tycoon.

The 40-tile board features 22 Pakistani cities, railway stations and utilities, with Qismat and Sandooq cards full of local flavour.

Choose from tokens like the Rickshaw, Truck, Cricket Bat, Chai Cup, Markhor and Crescent.

How to play

  1. Roll the dice and move around the board.
  2. Buy cities and collect them in colour groups.
  3. Build up your properties and collect rent.
  4. Bankrupt your rivals to win.
