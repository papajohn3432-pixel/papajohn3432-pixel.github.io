About this app

TestersHub helps indie developers find app testers, complete closed testing on Google Play and track their 14-day test in one place.

Submit your app with its closed-test opt-in link, choose what testers should focus on, and add a safety buffer of extra testers.

Test other developers' apps to earn Hub Credits, then spend them to get testers for your own app. Credits never reward ratings or reviews, only real testing.

Follow your test with a progress ring, day-by-day timeline, tester roster and daily check-ins.

How to play

  1. Submit your app with its closed-test link.
  2. Test other developers' apps and check in daily.
  3. Earn Hub Credits for completed tests.
  4. Spend credits to get testers and track your 14 days.
