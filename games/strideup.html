About this app

StrideUp is a step tracker and fitness companion that turns walking into daily progress.

See today's steps at a glance, follow your progress over time and earn awards as you hit goals.

Follow guided workouts and a simple plan, level up as you go, and keep your profile and streaks in one place.

Groups and friendly challenges help you stay motivated.

How to play

  1. Open the app and start walking.
  2. Check your steps on the Today screen.
  3. Follow a plan or workout to mix things up.
  4. Earn awards and level up as you improve.
