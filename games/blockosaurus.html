About this game

Blockosaurus is a free, offline block puzzle game with dinosaurs. Drag wood and colour blocks onto the board, clear rows and columns, and collect 12 real dinosaurs along the way.

There is no WiFi requirement and no time limit, so you can play on the bus, at home or anywhere you have a few quiet minutes.

Journey mode takes you through hundreds of stages with boss levels and 3-star goals. Classic is relaxed endless play, Daily Challenge gives everyone the same new puzzle each day, and Sudoku Mode adds 4x4 square clears.

Hatch dino eggs for gems, coins or new dinosaurs, then level your dinos up. Each one grants a power in play: Smash, Reroll, Rewind, Wave or Last Stand.

How to play

  1. Drag blocks onto the 8x8 board.
  2. Fill a full row or column to clear it.
  3. Clear several lines at once for combos and streak bonuses.
  4. Plan ahead, because the game ends when no block fits.
