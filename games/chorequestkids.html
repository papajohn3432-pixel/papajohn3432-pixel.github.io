About this app

Chore Quest Kids is a family chore chart and rewards app that turns daily jobs into a game. Kids finish chores, routines and a short daily lesson to earn coins and stars, then trade them for rewards the family chooses.

Parents set up chores, rewards and two PINs: one for parents and one for kids. Kids send photo proof, parents approve, and coins arrive instantly.

Morning and bedtime routines come with visual step-by-step timers and a stars-and-reward goal. A short daily lesson or story adds learning time.

An optional family account lets the whole household share one view of progress.

How to play

  1. Parents add chores and rewards, and set two PINs.
  2. Kids see their chore list and complete tasks.
  3. Kids send a photo as proof.
  4. Parents approve and coins are added for the reward store.
