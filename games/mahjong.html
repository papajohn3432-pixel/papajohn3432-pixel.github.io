About this game

Mahjong Solitaire: Tile Match is the classic tile-matching puzzle, reimagined with a calm garden setting and five real mahjong board layouts.

Clear the board one matching pair at a time. The difficulty ramps up gently, easing new players in instead of dropping them on the hardest board.

Play 1,000 levels across 5 phases, from a wide-open warm-up board to the full 144-tile Turtle layout, and collect 16 tile themes along the way.

Everything works offline, with free daily coins and a daily challenge to keep things fresh.

How to play

  1. Find two identical free tiles.
  2. Tap both to remove them.
  3. Clear every tile to finish the level.
  4. Use Hint, Shuffle or Undo if you get stuck.
