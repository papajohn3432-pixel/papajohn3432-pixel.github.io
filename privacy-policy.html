This Privacy Policy explains how the app Blockosaurus: Dino Puzzle ("Blockosaurus", "the app", "we", "us", or "our"), published on Google Play by the individual developer Babar Imran, handles information when you install and use it. 1. Summary

Blockosaurus: Dino Puzzle is a single‑player block puzzle game that works fully on your device and offline, with no account needed. We do not sell your personal information.

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose to, it enables cloud save and the online monthly leaderboard, provided by Google Firebase (see Section 3.4). If you never sign in, nothing about your game leaves your device except what Google's advertising and billing services process (Sections 3.1 and 3.2).

The app shows advertisements and offers optional in‑app purchases. Those features are provided by Google (Google AdMob and Google Play Billing), and Google processes a limited amount of device and usage data to make them work, as described below.

2. Information the app itself collects

Unless you choose to sign in (Section 3.4), none of it leaves your device.

The app stores the following locally on your device only (using the device's standard app‑storage / "shared preferences" mechanism), so that your game works between sessions:

  • Game progress: current stage, chapter, star ratings, endless‑mode best score, monthly best score.
  • Your in‑game currency ("gems") and unlocked items (themes, dinosaurs).
  • Achievement progress.
  • Settings: sound and music volume, selected difficulty mode, selected theme.

This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data from Android Settings. We have no ability to access it and keep no copy of it.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, microphone or camera input, files, or call/SMS logs.

3. Information collected by third‑party services

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / AD_ID).
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it.
  • Device information such as device model, operating system version, language, and general usage/diagnostic data.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit the number of times you see the same ad, detect invalid activity and fraud, and — depending on your device settings and consent — to personalise the ads you see. Google may act as an independent data controller for this data.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob / Google Ads data practices: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Your choices: - You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device). - Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), the app presents a Google‑certified consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings.

3.2 In‑app purchases — Google Play Billing

The app offers optional in‑app purchases (for example gem packs and an ad‑removal purchase). All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your credit/debit card number or other payment details. From a completed purchase we receive only a purchase token and product identifier from Google so the app can unlock what you bought and so purchases can be restored on a new device.

Google's handling of purchase data is governed by the Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=0.privacynotice

3.3 Google Play Services

Google Play distributes the app and provides core services (such as delivery, integrity checks, and crash collection at the platform level). This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app does not include Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics, or any social‑network, attribution, or third‑party analytics SDK.

3.4 Optional Google sign‑in, cloud save and leaderboard — Google Firebase

Only if you tap Continue with Google, the app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google LLC) to:

  • sign you in with your Google account and receive your name, email address, profile photo link and a unique account ID;
  • back up your game progress (stage progress, stars, collection, coins and gems, settings) so it can be restored on another device or after reinstalling;
  • publish your display name and best monthly score to the shared monthly leaderboard, which other signed‑in players can see. Your email address is not shown to other players.

This data is stored on Google's servers, encrypted in transit, and used only to provide these features. It is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Deleting it: in the app go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your cloud save and your leaderboard entry. You can also request deletion at https://papajohn3432-pixel.github.io/delete-account.html or by email (Section 12). Signing out keeps everything stored until you delete.

Firebase is Google's service; see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The third‑party services listed in Section 3 (Google AdMob and Google Play Billing) receive the data described there directly and process it under their own privacy policies. We do not receive that data from them in an identifiable form.

We may disclose information only if required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process — but in practice we hold no user data to disclose.
