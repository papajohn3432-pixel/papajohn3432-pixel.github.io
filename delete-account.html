Delete your Blockosaurus account

App: Blockosaurus: Dino Puzzle (com.babar.blockosaurus) · Developer: Babar Imran

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Blockosaurus and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, your cloud save and your leaderboard entry. If the app asks you to sign in again first, do so and repeat the steps — it is a security check.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email papajohn3432@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Blockosaurus account”. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your Google account name, email address, profile photo link and account ID as stored by the app.
  • Your cloud save (game progress, collection, coins and gems, settings).
  • Your monthly leaderboard entry and display name.

Game progress stored only on your phone is not affected — uninstalling the app removes it. Purchases are handled by Google Play and stay in your Google account. Advertising data is held by Google; see Google’s privacy policy.
