Privacy Policy

Numvia: AI Math Solver · Developer: Babar Imran · Last updated: 21 September 2026

Numvia helps students understand maths: scan or type a problem, see step-by-step solutions, ask an AI tutor, practise and track progress. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • Your account, progress, history and settings are stored on your device.
  • Photos you scan are sent securely to be read, then discarded. We do not save your photos.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.
  • You can delete everything in the app at any time (Settings → Privacy).

Permissions

  • Camera – only when you scan a problem.
  • Microphone – only when you use voice input.
  • Notifications – optional study reminders you can switch off in Settings.
  • Photos / files – only the image you pick.

AI-generated content

Explanations, practice questions and tutor replies are generated by AI and can occasionally be wrong. Check important answers, and use the flag button on any solution or tutor reply to report a problem. Exact algebra (linear and quadratic equations) is solved on your device with a maths engine.

Children

Numvia is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you delete the data.

Sharing

We share problem text and images only with the AI service needed to answer you, and purchase status with Google Play / the App Store. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it: use Settings → Privacy → Delete account, or uninstall the app. See how to delete your data.

Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with a random salt. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit what leaves your device to what is needed to answer you.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
