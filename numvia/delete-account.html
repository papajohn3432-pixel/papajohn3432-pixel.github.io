Delete your Numvia data

Numvia: AI Math Solver · Developer: Babar Imran · Last updated: 21 September 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Numvia and go to Profile → Settings → Privacy.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently erases your account, progress, history, saved items, settings and scheduled reminders from your device.

Delete only your scan history

Privacy → Delete scan history removes every solved problem while keeping your account.

If you can no longer open the app

Uninstalling Numvia removes all data stored on the device. Because your account and progress live on your device, we hold no copy to delete. If you want confirmation, or you contacted us about a report, email Papajohn3432@gmail.com and we will delete anything we hold within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or the App Store subscription settings.

What we keep

Photos and messages are not stored after an answer is returned. Reports you chose to send may be kept for up to 12 months to improve accuracy and safety.
